What to look for

  • Lantern off: the red slat light should reach him only as a backlit edge and on the floor, with no stripes crossing his body.
  • A two-frame breathing idle, so he is never perfectly still. The lantern flicker also moves across him.
  • Turn: the face, hair and build should stay the same person from every facing, and the lighting should stay consistent as he turns.
  • Player highlight is the accessibility option, off by default.
